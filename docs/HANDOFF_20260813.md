## 5. The findings that matter

### 5.1 The champion's deployment artifact was one season stale

`member_fingerprint` puts B1-S at `0.5352282203`, the shipped champion at
`0.5401750413`. Both are `fpipe['priors']['asof_pitcher_success_rate']`, and
both were checked against the data to ten places:

```
mean asof_pitcher_success_rate over all rows (<=2024)  0.5352282203   B1-S
mean over <= 2023                                      0.5401750413   champion
```

So v11's inference-time features were built through 2023 while its trees were
refit through 2024. Two-stage fixes it. **This difference is expected — do not
chase it as an error.**

### 5.2 Constants fitted on one season lose to the ones already in the script

Three independent measurements, same shape:

| | in-sample / oracle | fitted on prior season, frozen |
|---|---:|---:|
| calibration `sigmoid(a·logit+b)` | +34.56 (isotonic oracle) | **−19.93** vs legacy −1.22 |
| recent-middle 8-bin lookup | +17.40 oracle | +5.72 vs **shipped +8.03** |
| pitcher×batter offsets | **+436.28** oracle | **+3.80** |

Plus the historical instance: v12 changed the middle lookup and lost LB −18.271.

**`model/matchup_constants_2024.json` records `pb_raw_increment: 425.73`.** That
number is in-sample. Out of sample the step is worth **+3.80** — only 38.1% of
2024 rows match a pitcher-batter pair seen in 2023. Anyone reading 425.73 as
headroom is off by 112×.

Also: the legacy slope contributes nothing. On B1-S, pure centering scores
956.15 against legacy slope+shift's 956.14. All +26.65 is bias removal.

### 5.3 The three P1 fixes are strongly non-additive

Core Δ vs `B0JL`, judging surface, seeds 3/4/5:

```
--te-fit-prior         -0.01      --two-stage-artifact   +1.01
--skill-neutral-first  -5.59      sum of the three       -4.59
--p1 (all three)       +1.12      interaction            +5.71
```

Do not attribute a bundled result by subtraction. I did, twice, and was wrong
both times — first blaming the TE prior and season drift (the drift numbers were
right, the causal claim was not), then estimating two-stage's share.

## 7. Two silent-failure bugs caught today (both would have reported a clean null)

1. **Expected-pitch-mix was a byte copy of the personal rate.** Trackman spells
   hands `"Right"`/`"Left"`, `train.csv` codes them `2`/`1`. Every context lookup
   missed, the ratio came back 1.0 everywhere, correlation 1.000000. The run
   would have trained, scored, and reported "no effect". Both
   `build_expected_mix.py` and `--extra-feats` now refuse above a miss threshold.
2. **`make_matchup_constants.py` joined predictions to train rows by position.**
   Only the row count was checked. Now joins on `row_id`, asserts the exact seed
   set, and writes to `model/` (the script reads `model/`, the generator wrote
   `out/` — a manual copy step in the middle).

  `desktop-4070` and `hsu-server` are down (the 4070 is the A100's ProxyJump).

  Put chains in a `.bat`.
